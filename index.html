Guess or Mess - THE SERPENT WHEEL…

Rules of Play

1
Each team selects a question number from the board.
2
The team guesses an alphabet based on their judgement.
3
Once an alphabet is selected, every box holding that alphabet opens at once. 5 wrong chances are allowed per team.
4
Vowels — A E I O U — may be opted only on payment of a premium price of 200 points.
5
If the alphabet guessed is correct, the team gets to spin the wheel and earns points accordingly.
6
On the wheel the point system is straightforward, but — SNAKE nullifies the team's entire score; PASS hands the question, in its present state, to the immediate next team.
7
At any point, a team that does not wish to play further may choose to discontinue and secure the points earned up to that stage.
